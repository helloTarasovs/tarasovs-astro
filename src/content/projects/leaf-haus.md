<p>Leaf Haus came to us needing a complete website rebuild — a new WordPress site designed from scratch and integrated with Dispense (now rebranded as AIQ Ecommerce), the storefront platform powering their online menu.</p>
<p>The build included a full product category system (flower, pre-rolls, vaporizers, concentrates, edibles, accessories), a “Cannabis 101” education hub — strain guides, terpene profiles, a cannabis glossary, and consumption-method breakdowns — and a blog system built for ongoing content.</p>
<p>Beyond the initial build and launch, we stayed on for about a year as their ongoing web and SEO partner: writing SEO-focused blog content — brand reviews, strain guides, and local cannabis news — rolling out design updates, and building new pages as the business grew.</p>
<p><em>Leaf Haus has since transitioned to a different local team, and the site has been redesigned since our involvement — this case study reflects the design, build, and SEO work completed during our engagement.</em></p>
