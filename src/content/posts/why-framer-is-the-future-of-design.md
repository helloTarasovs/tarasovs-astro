<p>In the fast-paced world of web design and development, new tools emerge every year - but only a few redefine how we build digital experiences. <strong>Framer</strong> is one of them. Combining design freedom with real interactivity, Framer is quickly becoming the go-to platform for designers and developers who want to <strong>create stunning websites, prototypes, and landing pages</strong> - without writing endless lines of code.</p><p>In this article, we’ll explore why <strong>Framer is the future of web design</strong>, and how it’s transforming the creative workflow for professionals in Europe and around the world.</p>
<h2>What is Framer?</h2>
<p>Framer is a <strong>no-code/low-code design tool</strong> that lets you build fully functional websites with beautiful animations, interactions, and responsiveness. Unlike traditional design tools (like Figma or Sketch), Framer bridges the gap between <strong>design and development</strong>, offering a real-time, live-preview environment powered by React.</p><p>It’s not just for prototyping - it’s for <strong>publishing real websites</strong>, instantly.</p>

Perfect for UX designers who want to showcase real user flows.</p><h3>2. <strong>Powered by React</strong></h3><p>Framer runs on React under the hood, which means it’s not just a toy - it’s capable of <strong>building scalable, production-grade components</strong>.
