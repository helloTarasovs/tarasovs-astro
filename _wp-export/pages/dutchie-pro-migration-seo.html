Two starting points. One deadline.

If you’re on Dutchie Plus (headless), you have a custom storefront rendering real, ranked product URLs — and it’s being deprecated. When the API stops responding, those URLs 404 and every ranking signal you built over 12–24 months collapses at once. Dutchie supports legacy Plus through the end of 2026; after that, don’t assume a dependable path. If you’re on the standard Dutchie iFrame, your menu loads from Dutchie’s domain inside a frame — so your products are close to invisible in search today. You’re not losing rankings in a migration; you never had them. Either way the destination is the same: Dutchie Pro, which serves store and product pages as crawlable URLs on your own domain.

    Dutchie Pro migration FAQ

    When is Dutchie Plus being sunset?
    Dutchie has said it will support legacy Dutchie Plus customers through the end of 2026. Exact end-of-life dates are tier-dependent, so request a written timeline from your Dutchie account manager and plan to complete migration well before the API stops responding.We checked Dutchie's own Help Center, release notes, and product pages before publishing this — none of them list a public cutoff date or deprecation schedule. The end-of-2026 date and any specific timeline you see quoted elsewhere comes from account-level communication, not a published company announcement. Treat it as unconfirmed until your account manager puts a date in writing
    What's the difference between the Dutchie iFrame and Dutchie Pro?

    The default iFrame embeds your menu from Dutchie's domain inside a frame, which search engines struggle to index as real pages. Dutchie Pro serves store and product pages as standalone, crawlable URLs on your own domain, so each can be found, ranked, and cited individually.

    Will migrating to Dutchie Pro hurt my SEO?

    Only if it's done without proper URL mapping and redirects. The real risks are URL changes without 301s, dropped metadata, broken internal links, and stale schema. We map every indexed URL before migration and preserve structure wherever possible — which is why Verdi's rankings grew through the migration instead of dropping.

    How long does a Dutchie Pro migration take to show results?

    Indexing of newly crawlable pages typically begins within the first few weeks. Meaningful ranking and traffic gains build over 3–6 months as Google gains confidence in the new URLs — consistent with the six-month window behind the Verdi results.

    Do I have to leave Dutchie to fix my menu SEO?

    No. If you're happy with Dutchie operationally, migrating to Dutchie Pro keeps your POS, staff, and customer accounts while making your menu indexable. Switching providers only makes sense if you were already planning to leave for other reasons.

    Can you migrate a multi-location / MSO setup?

    Yes. Multi-location migrations need more URL mapping and per-location QA, and we scope the timeline accordingly. Store-location pages are usually among the biggest winners, since each becomes an indexable, locally relevant page instead of a menu tab.
