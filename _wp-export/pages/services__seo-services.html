Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most websites start seeing measurable ranking improvements within 3 to 6 months. Competitive niches or websites with significant technical issues may take longer. SEO compounds over time — the earlier you start, the stronger your position becomes.

Do you work with businesses outside New York?

Yes. We serve clients across the United States, United Kingdom, and Europe. Our work is fully remote, and we have experience targeting both US and European search markets.

What makes your SEO different from other agencies?

We do not separate SEO from web development. When we identify a technical issue, we fix it. When a page needs better content, we write it. Most agencies stop at recommendations — we execute.

Do you offer one-time audits?

Yes. If you need a comprehensive SEO audit without an ongoing retainer, we offer standalone technical and content audits. Contact us to discuss the scope.

Can SEO help me appear in ChatGPT or Google AI answers?

Yes — this is what we call GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). It requires structured content, strong authority signals, and FAQ schema. We integrate GEO into our SEO process across all packages.

How do you report progress?

Every month you receive a report covering keyword rankings, organic traffic, technical issue status, backlink growth, and the plan for the next 30 days. Strategy calls are held at a frequency matched to your package.

What do you need from us to get started?

Access to your Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, and a call to align on your business goals and target audience. Everything else we handle.
