Website Development FAQ

Which website development platforms do you work with?

We develop websites in Framer, Webflow, and WordPress. We choose the platform based on your goals, content requirements, integrations, and how your team plans to manage the website.

How do you choose the right platform for a project?

Framer is often the best fit for high-performance marketing websites, portfolios, and startup launches. Webflow works well for scalable CMS websites and complex interactions, while WordPress is suitable for projects that require plugins, custom post types, WooCommerce, or an established content infrastructure.

What is included in every website development project?

Every development project includes clean semantic HTML, Core Web Vitals optimization, a mobile-first responsive build, GEO-ready content architecture, structured headings, schema markup, cross-browser testing, and developer handoff documentation.

Will my website be optimized for SEO and AI search?

Yes. We structure websites so that search engines and AI-powered platforms can understand the content accurately. This includes semantic HTML, clear heading structures, fast performance, FAQ sections, structured content, and relevant schema markup.

Do you build responsive websites?

Yes. Every website is developed using a mobile-first approach and tested across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes, as well as modern web browsers.

Can my team manage the website after launch?

Yes. We configure the CMS around your content and provide handoff documentation so your team can update pages, publish content, and manage the website independently after launch.

Do you develop e-commerce websites?

Yes. For projects that require online selling, product management, and advanced e-commerce functionality, we can develop a WordPress website with WooCommerce when it is the right platform for the project.

Who do you provide website development services for?

We work with startups launching their first website, established companies replacing underperforming platforms, and agencies that need a reliable development partner. We serve businesses in New York, across the United States, and throughout Europe.
