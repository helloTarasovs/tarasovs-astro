What is GEO and why does it matter in 2026?

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — is the practice of structuring website content so that AI-powered platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews cite your brand in their generated responses. In 2026, AI search is one of the fastest-growing discovery channels. Businesses that appear in AI answers receive high-intent referrals at no cost per click. We integrate GEO into every service we offer.
