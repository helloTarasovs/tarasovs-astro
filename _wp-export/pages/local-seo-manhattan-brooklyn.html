The 2026 Google Business Profile update changed the game for dispensaries

Q&A is gone. Google retired user-facing Q&A; Gemini now auto-answers common questions from your services catalog, posts, photos, reviews, and indexed website content — so those signals matter more than ever. Stock and AI images are banned and now act as audit triggers that can suppress your listing. A new 30-day photo-freshness rule replaced vague “post regularly” advice with a measurable cadence — “set it and forget it” profiles lose Map Pack position. For dispensaries this hits harder than for anyone else: with no paid ads to fall back on, a GBP scoring change that costs a normal retailer a few percent of visibility can cost a dispensary 30–40%.     Local SEO FAQ

    Can cannabis dispensaries even show up on Google Maps?

    Yes. A Google Business Profile is the main way licensed dispensaries appear in Maps and the local pack. The rules are stricter than for other businesses, so it has to be optimized carefully — but done right, it's your highest-intent local channel.

    What changed with the 2026 Google Business Profile update?

    Google retired user-facing Q&A (Gemini now auto-answers from your profile and website), banned stock and AI images as suppression triggers, and added a 30-day photo-freshness rule. For dispensaries with no paid-ads backstop, these changes affect local visibility much more than for typical retailers.

    How do you rank in the Google Map Pack?

    Google weighs proximity, relevance, and prominence. We optimize your profile completeness and categories, build local landing pages, generate reviews, keep photos fresh, and fix citation consistency — the signals that decide the top three local results.

    Do I need separate pages for each neighborhood?

    For a competitive market or multiple service areas, yes. Dedicated neighborhood pages capture searches like “park slope dispensary” or “Chelsea weed delivery” that a single generic locations page can't rank for.

    Will optimizing my Google Business Profile risk a suspension?

    Only if it's done without the cannabis-specific rules. We follow Google's requirements on categories, pricing, promotions, and imagery so your listing stays live — protecting it is part of the work, not an afterthought.

    How fast does local SEO work for a dispensary?

    Map Pack and Google Business Profile improvements often show within the first 30–60 days, since local signals update faster than broad organic rankings. Your free audit flags the quickest wins first.

    How does local SEO connect to AI search?

    AI assistants build local recommendations from the same signals as Google — a complete profile, reviews, fresh photos, and crawlable location pages. Strong local SEO is what makes your dispensary eligible to be the name an assistant recommends nearby.
