Questions New York dispensaries ask

Do you only work with dispensaries in New York City?

No. We work with licensed dispensaries across New York State — Long Island, the Hudson Valley, the Capital Region, Western New York, Rochester, Syracuse, and the Southern Tier. If you are in the five boroughs, our NYC dispensary SEO page covers that market specifically.

Is SEO for an upstate dispensary different from NYC?

Yes, meaningfully. In the city, proximity dominates and you are competing with dozens of stores in the same few blocks. Upstate, customers search across a 15 to 25 minute drive, competition is thinner, and basics like a properly built Google Business Profile and an indexable menu can move you into the Map Pack within a couple of months.

We have stores in more than one region. How does that work?

One domain, one authority base, a separate page per location with genuinely local content, clean and consistent NAP data, and a Google Business Profile managed per store. Reporting is broken out by location so you can see which market is actually producing orders.

Can dispensaries actually do SEO if they can't advertise?

Yes — and that's exactly why it matters more for you than for most businesses. Google Ads and Meta do not accept cannabis retail advertising, and New York adds its own restrictions on audience and placement. Organic search, Google Maps, and AI search are your main channels for reaching new customers.

Is this compliant? Will it put my listings or license at risk?

Compliance comes first. We avoid health claims and the content patterns that get listings flagged or suspended, we work within New York's marketing and advertising rules on audience and placement, and we follow the cannabis-specific rules for Google Business Profile. Protecting your listings is part of the job, not an afterthought.

Do you build a separate page for every town?

Only where there is real demand and real content to put on the page. Mass-produced city pages with a swapped town name are doorway pages, and Google has been demoting them for years. We build fewer pages that actually rank.

Can you optimize our Dutchie menu for SEO?

Yes. We audit how your Dutchie menu connects to your website, whether menu, category, and product pages are crawlable and indexed, and how they support local and organic search. We also handle Dutchie Pro migrations without losing rankings.

What is GEO / AI search optimization?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is structuring your content and data so AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI — cite and recommend your dispensary when people ask. Those answers name two or three businesses rather than ten links, and almost no dispensary in New York is optimized for it yet. More on GEO services.

How fast will I see results?

Local and Google Maps improvements often show within the first 30 to 60 days, and usually faster in less competitive upstate markets than in the city. AI-search visibility builds over a few weeks to a couple of months as content and trust signals take hold.

Do you help with Dutchie Pro migration?

Yes. We help dispensaries plan SEO-safe migrations from Dutchie Plus, Dutchie iFrame, or legacy Dutchie setups to Dutchie Pro: URL planning, indexation checks, Google Search Console setup, GA4 tracking, redirect planning, technical SEO review, and post-launch monitoring.

What does it cost?

Engagements are monthly programs scoped to your market and number of locations. The best place to start is the free visibility audit — it shows what's needed before you commit to anything.
