What is GEO
Generative Engine Optimization?

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — is the practice of structuring and positioning website content so that AI-powered search engines, including ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot, cite your brand in their generated responses. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets blue-link rankings on a search results page, GEO targets the AI layer above those results — where users now receive direct answers without clicking through to websites. The goal of GEO is to become a trusted, cited source inside those AI-generated answers.

GEO encompasses five core disciplines: content authority building, structured data implementation, E-E-A-T optimization, prompt-aligned FAQ creation, and brand citation strategy across authoritative third-party sources.

Frequently asked questions about GEO

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — is the discipline of optimizing website content, structured data, and brand authority signals so that AI-powered search engines — including ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot — cite your brand in their generated responses. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets search engine rankings, GEO targets the AI layer that now answers a growing share of user queries directly, without
requiring a website click.

How is GEO different from SEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for keyword rankings in Google's blue-link results. GEO optimizes for citations in AI-generated answers. The two disciplines share some foundations — quality content, technical site health, authority signals — but GEO requires additional practices: structured content architecture for AI retrieval, FAQPage schema, prompt-aligned content, E-E-A-T authority building, and Share of Model measurement. In 2026, businesses need
both.

How long does GEO take to show results?

First AI citations typically appear within 4–8 weeks for well-optimized content in low-to-medium competition topics. New content enters AI citation pools within 3–5 business days on most platforms, though competitive queries take longer to penetrate. Share of Model improvements become measurable within the first 60 days. Sustained authority in competitive niches typically takes 3–6 months, similar to traditional SEO timelines.

How do you measure GEO results?

We measure GEO through Share of Model (SoM) — the percentage of relevant AI queries where your brand is cited. Each month, we test a predefined set of 50–100 queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Copilot, and document where your brand appears, in what position, and with what context. We also track citation growth on Clutch, industry publications, and other third-party sources that feed AI model training.

Can GEO work alongside my existing SEO strategy?

Yes — and it works better when it does. GEO and traditional SEO share the same technical foundation: fast-loading, crawlable sites with strong content and authority signals. The GEO-specific additions — structured content, FAQ schema, E-E-A-T optimization — also improve traditional SEO performance. Tarasovs manages both disciplines in an integrated strategy so they reinforce rather than duplicate each other.

What is GEO and why is it included in your web design projects?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring website content so that AI-powered search platforms — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity — cite your business in their generated answers. We include GEO-ready content structure in every project because AI search is already where a significant portion of user attention goes in 2026.

Which AI platforms does GEO target?

Tarasovs GEO strategy targets the major AI search and answer platforms in 2026: ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot. Each platform has different retrieval and citation behavior, so our strategy accounts for platform-specific optimization techniques.

What is Share of Model and how do you track it?

Share of Model (SoM) is the percentage of relevant AI queries in your industry where your brand is mentioned or cited. It is the GEO equivalent of keyword rankings — the primary metric for measuring AI search visibility. Tarasovs tracks SoM monthly by testing your defined query set across all major AI platforms, documenting brand appearances, competitor presence, and citation context. We report SoM changes monthly alongside the specific content changes that drove improvements.

Why is my website not showing up in ChatGPT or Perplexity?

The most common reasons are: AI crawlers are blocked by your robots.txt or CDN configuration; your content lacks the structured Q&A format that AI retrieval systems prefer; your site has low E-E-A-T signals and is not recognized as an authoritative source; your brand is not mentioned on independent third-party domains that AI models use for corroboration; or your content is rendered client-side in JavaScript, which many AI bots cannot process. A GEO audit identifies which factors apply to your site and prioritizes fixes by impact.
