5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and collect anonymised usage data.

Cookies we use

Category Cookie name / source Purpose Duration
Strictly necessary WordPress session, PHPSESSID Site functionality, security, form submission Session
Preferences wp-settings-* Remembers your display preferences 1 year
Analytics _ga, _gid, _gat (Google Analytics) Tracks page views and user behaviour anonymously Up to 2 years
Marketing _fbp (Facebook Pixel, if active) Measures ad performance and campaign reach 90 days

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling analytics or marketing cookies will not affect your ability to browse the site. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality.

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your personal data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Right to restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@tarasovs.me. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
