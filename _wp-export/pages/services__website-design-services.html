FAQ

How much does a website design cost with Tarasovs?

Website design pricing depends on the scope, number of pages, and functionality required. A professional business website typically starts from $3,000. E-commerce and custom web applications start from $6,000. We provide a detailed proposal after our discovery call.

How long does it take to design and launch a website?

Most business websites take 6–10 weeks from kickoff to launch. E-commerce projects typically take 8–14 weeks. Timeline depends on scope, content readiness, and how quickly feedback rounds are completed.

Do you design websites for businesses outside New York?
Yes. We regularly work with clients across the United States and Europe, including the UK, Germany, the Netherlands, and Romania. Our team is distributed across multiple time zones, which makes remote collaboration smooth.
What platforms do you build on?

We work with Framer, Webflow, WordPress, Shopify and custom-built solutions depending on your needs. We recommend the platform that best fits your content management style, budget, and scalability requirements.

Is SEO included in the website design?

Every site we build includes technical SEO foundations: clean code, optimized page speed, proper heading structure, meta tags, schema markup, and sitemap. Ongoing SEO and content strategy are available as a separate service.

What is GEO and why is it included in your web design projects?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring website content so that AI-powered search platforms — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity — cite your business in their generated answers. We include GEO-ready content structure in every project because AI search is already where a significant portion of user attention goes in 2026.

Do you provide website maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance plans that include software updates, security monitoring, performance checks, and content edits. Details are included in every project proposal.
