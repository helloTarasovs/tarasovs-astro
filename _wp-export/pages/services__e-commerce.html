Your product pages
may be invisible to Google

Most e-commerce platforms make it easy to add a catalog and hard to know whether that catalog actually gets indexed. Embedded menus, iFrame widgets, and JavaScript-rendered product grids often display fine in a browser and return nothing to a search crawler. The store owner sees products on the page and assumes everything is working. Google sees an empty container.

The same problem now extends to AI. If a language model can’t read your product data, it can’t recommend your products when someone asks for a suggestion. A catalog invisible to Google today is a catalog invisible to ChatGPT and Perplexity tomorrow.

The check is simple, even if the fix isn’t. Right-click any product page, choose “View Page Source,” and search for the product name and price. If they’re not in that raw HTML, they’re being injected by JavaScript after the page loads, and a large share of crawlers and AI agents will never see them.

We saw this directly with Verdi Cannabis: their Dutchie embedded menu meant every product page returned nothing to a crawler. We rebuilt the URL structure and migrated the integration to Dutchie Pro, which exposes crawlable product pages instead of an opaque widget. Read the full breakdown in the Verdi Cannabis case study.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms do you build on?

Shopify and WooCommerce for full stores. For businesses using a third-party catalog like Dutchie, we work with the platform you're on and fix how it connects to your site's SEO.

Why aren't my product pages showing up in Google?

The most common cause is an embedded catalog, an iFrame or JavaScript widget that renders products after the page loads. Google often doesn't see those pages at all. A crawl audit will tell you within a day whether this applies to you, usually by checking whether your product data appears in the page's raw HTML.

Can you fix SEO for an embedded menu or third-party catalog?

Yes. This is one of our specialisms. We audit how the catalog connects to your domain, whether category and product pages are crawlable, and restructure so they support organic search instead of sitting outside it, as we did for Verdi Cannabis on Dutchie.

We're replatforming. Will we lose our rankings?

Not if the migration is planned. We handle URL mapping, redirect planning, indexation checks, Search Console and GA4 setup, and post-launch monitoring for several weeks after launch. Rankings drop when redirects are an afterthought, not when they're planned.

Do you do e-commerce SEO as well as development?

Yes, and we prefer to do both. When SEO is planned during the build, the structure is right from day one instead of being retrofitted.

What is e-commerce GEO?

Structuring product data, category descriptions, and FAQs so AI assistants can cite your store when someone asks for a product recommendation. Most stores have zero presence in AI answers today.

Do you work with international stores?

Yes. We handle multi-language setups, hreflang, and opening an existing catalog to new markets, as we did for ShoppingModaItalia.
