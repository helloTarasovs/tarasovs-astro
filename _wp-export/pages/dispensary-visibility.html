Why this exists

AI does not rank you lower. It leaves you out of the answer.

Across the first 100 New York dispensaries we measured, 31 scored under 40 and nine were not named in a single one of their ten recorded prompts. Not ranked low. Absent.

The pattern is not specific to cannabis. SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index analyzed more than 350,000 business locations: Google's local 3-pack surfaced 35.9% of them, ChatGPT 1.2%. In retail, only 45% of the brands leading Google's local results also appeared in AI recommendations.

The result is not a bell curve

Half the dispensaries measured are visible. Roughly a third are close to invisible. The middle is thin. ChatGPT is not ranking these businesses along a gradient, it is sorting most of them into recommended or not recommended.

Manhattan is the weakest market we have measured

Across 18 measured Manhattan dispensaries the median composite is 36. Across the rest of the state it is 70. Manhattan also holds the highest scores in the entire Index, which is the point: a handful of well-known names absorb the recommendations and the rest are not named at all.

Density appears to concentrate visibility rather than spread it. Boroughs with fewer than ten measured profiles are not compared here, because the samples are too small to say anything.

Being first at home is not the same as being visible

Eleven dispensaries scored exactly 70: first in all six prompts in their own market, absent from all four prompts covering the surrounding and wider markets. On a single number that reads as healthy visibility. In practice it means only customers who already name the neighbourhood ever see them.

This is why every profile carries a visibility pattern alongside the composite.

These figures describe the 100 dispensaries measured so far, not all 705 licensed retailers in New York, and the sample is not random: it reflects the order in which profiles were benchmarked. Figures are restated as coverage grows.

The benchmark

What ChatGPT Visibility measures

The Index tests whether ChatGPT recommends a dispensary when users ask common non-branded local discovery questions — the kind a customer actually types before deciding where to shop.

Mention and position

Whether the dispensary appears at all, and where it sits in the recommendation order.

Consistency across prompts

Appearing once is noise. The benchmark uses a fixed set of ten prompts to separate reliable visibility from a lucky hit.

Geographic reach

Visibility in the immediate neighborhood, the surrounding area, and the wider metro, scored separately.

Competitors surfaced

Which dispensaries ChatGPT names when the business in question is missing from the answer.

Prompt-level detail

Each of the ten prompts is published with its own result, so the score can be checked rather than taken on trust.

Change over time

Scoring

How the composite ChatGPT Visibility score works

10 recorded prompts. 3 market levels. 1 composite score. Each market level is averaged separately, then combined by weight, so the market where a dispensary competes hardest for customers counts the most.

Market weighting

Each market level is scored as the average of its own prompts, then the three level scores are combined by weight. This gives the greatest weight to the market where the dispensary most directly competes for customers.

70%Primary Market — average of 6 prompts in the dispensary's own neighborhood
20%Secondary Market — average of 2 prompts in the surrounding borough or district
10%Regional Market — average of 2 prompts in the largest relevant city or metro

Composite ChatGPT Visibility = Primary Market × 70% + Secondary Market × 20% + Regional Market × 10%, on a 0–100 scale.

10 recorded prompts → 3 market levels → recommendation position → composite score, 0–100.

About the benchmark

The New York Dispensary Visibility Index is a search visibility benchmark built and maintained by Tarasovs Digital Agency, an SEO agency that also sells dispensary search services. We think that is worth stating plainly rather than burying: the data is collected the same way for every business, including the ones we work with.

Each of the 10 non-branded prompts is entered in a separate new ChatGPT Temporary Chat with no previous benchmark conversation history. For every prompt we record the returned response, the observed recommendation position, the market being tested and the competing dispensaries named in that same response.

All 10 prompts must return a valid result before a public score is published. Results are stored as a dated benchmark, because ChatGPT recommendations can change as models, search results and available web information change.

Scores come from directly observed Temporary Chat results. Web results, directories and our own judgment are not used to reconstruct or estimate a recorded position. If a response was not captured, the profile stays Pending rather than being filled in.

A ranked position is only assigned when it is clearly identifiable in the response. Where a dispensary is mentioned but no clear Top-5 order can be identified, it is scored as mentioned / unranked rather than guessed at.

A high composite score does not represent business quality, product quality, customer satisfaction or revenue.

Google Search, Google Maps, Gemini and other AI platforms are not included in the public composite score.

Benchmark note: These are standardized Temporary Chat checks, not a prediction of what every user will see. ChatGPT responses vary by date, model, location, available sources and wording. Read a profile as a dated snapshot, not a permanent ranking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the New York Dispensary Visibility Index?

It is a database measuring how New York cannabis dispensaries appear in ChatGPT recommendations across a standardized set of local discovery prompts. Each published dispensary gets a public profile with its score, prompt-by-prompt results and the competitors ChatGPT surfaced.

What is ChatGPT Visibility?

ChatGPT Visibility measures whether a dispensary appears in ChatGPT recommendations and where it is positioned within the answer. It is scored 0–100 by weighting three market-level averages: 70% primary market, 20% secondary market and 10% regional market.

Why isn't my dispensary showing up in ChatGPT?

Usually it is not a judgment about the business. AI systems leave out businesses they cannot verify confidently. Inconsistent name, address and phone data across directories, thin or unclaimed listings, low review response rates and no third-party editorial coverage are the common causes. A dispensary can rank well on Google and still fail that confidence threshold.

Where does ChatGPT get local business information from?

ChatGPT Search can combine information from the open web and third-party data providers when generating local recommendations. The exact source mix can vary by query, location and product experience, which is one reason we measure the resulting recommendation itself rather than assuming visibility from any single directory.

Does a good Google ranking mean good ChatGPT visibility?

No. In SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index, only 45% of retail brands leading Google's local results also appeared in AI recommendations. The two run on different inputs and should be measured separately.

How many prompts are tested?

Ten standardized recommendation prompts per dispensary: six in the Primary Market, two in the Secondary Market and two in the Regional Market. Each prompt is run and the resulting ChatGPT response is recorded, so every result reflects an actual recommendation appearance rather than an inferred one.

What does "Not mentioned" mean?

The dispensary did not appear in the ChatGPT response recorded for that specific prompt on the check date. It scores zero points for that prompt.

Why is my profile marked Pending?

A public composite score is published only once all 10 standardized prompts return a valid result. If a request fails, times out, or cannot be validated, the profile stays Pending until the missing check completes successfully — we do not publish a score from a partial scan.

How is this different from Weedmaps or Leafly rankings?

Weedmaps and Leafly rank dispensaries inside their own platforms, using their own listings and reviews. The Index does not rank anything itself. It records what ChatGPT says when asked a neutral question, and reports the result.

Is Gemini included?

No. The current public benchmark covers ChatGPT only. Other AI providers may be published as separate datasets once their data is reliable enough to report.

Are Google rankings included?

Not in the public score. Google Organic and Google Maps analysis is available through the Full Visibility Report.

Is Menu SEO included in the public score?

No. Menu SEO can be analyzed as part of the Full Visibility Report but is not part of the public composite score.

How often are results updated?

Profiles are rechecked periodically because AI recommendations change. Every profile shows the date of its most recent check.

My score looks wrong. Can I get it rechecked?

Yes. Request a recheck through the Full Visibility Report form and we will re-run the standardized prompt set and update the profile with a new check date.

Who runs the New York Dispensary Visibility Index?

Tarasovs Digital Agency, a remote-first agency serving New York and US clients. The agency also sells dispensary SEO services, which is disclosed on this page. Every dispensary is measured with the same prompt set and the same scoring, and profiles are published whether the score is high or low.

Geographic coverage

A statewide view, not only New York City

The index includes eligible published profiles from New York City and from regions across the state. The geography controls are generated from the same live profile set as the ranking: NYC and Upstate appear only when those areas have matching profiles, and the Region menu lists only regions represented in the current published sample.

Use the All NY, NYC, Upstate and Region controls to narrow the results without changing the underlying benchmark. Regional comparisons become more useful as coverage grows; a region with fewer published profiles should not be interpreted as stronger or weaker solely from its sample size.

Research design

Complete methodology

ChatGPT Visibility measures how prominently each dispensary appeared in 10 recorded, non-branded ChatGPT recommendation checks across three geographic market levels.

Independent Temporary Chat protocol

  1. Each standardized prompt is entered in a separate new ChatGPT Temporary Chat with no previous benchmark conversation history.
  2. The returned response, observed recommendation position, market being tested and competing dispensaries surfaced in that response are recorded.
  3. Six prompts test the primary market, two prompts test the secondary market and two prompts test the regional or wider market.
  4. A public score is calculated only after all 10 standardized checks have a documented result.

Geographic weighting

The benchmark first calculates a score for each market level. The composite score then combines those three results using a fixed geographic weighting.

Composite score = Primary market 70% + Secondary market 20% + Regional/wider market 10%

Validation and inclusion

A public score is not calculated from a partial benchmark. The benchmark is READY only when all 10 standardized checks have a documented result. If any recorded response is missing or cannot be classified, the profile remains Pending until the full 10-check benchmark is complete.

The statistics and ranking include only published READY profiles. Drafts, incomplete benchmarks and profiles without a qualifying public result are excluded.

Benchmark source and limitations

This index uses directly observed Temporary Chat results: 10 separately recorded responses per published profile and the fixed 70% / 20% / 10% geographic weighting. Web results and directories are not used to reconstruct or estimate the recorded ChatGPT positions.

Benchmark note: The index records standardized Temporary Chat checks rather than predicting what every user will see. ChatGPT responses can vary by date, model, location, available sources and wording. The score does not represent overall business quality, product quality, customer satisfaction or revenue performance.
Scope: This edition measures ChatGPT only. It does not combine ChatGPT results with Google Maps, Google organic search, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is each dispensary measured?

Each published profile uses 10 standardized, non-branded recommendation prompts entered in 10 separate new ChatGPT Temporary Chats. The benchmark records the returned response, observed position, market and surfaced competitors.

How is the ChatGPT Visibility score calculated?

Recorded results receive 100 points for #1, 85 for #2, 70 for #3, 55 for #4–5, 40 for an unranked mention and 0 for no mention. Market scores are combined using 70% Primary, 20% Secondary and 10% Regional weighting.

What does “invisible” mean in this index?

A dispensary is classified as invisible when it receives no mentions across the complete standardized 10-prompt ChatGPT benchmark.

Why can a dispensary's score change?

ChatGPT responses can vary by date, model, location, available sources and wording. A new benchmark snapshot may therefore produce a different set or order of recommendations.

Why is a Pending profile excluded from the ranking?

A public score is published only after all 10 standardized checks have a documented result. A partial benchmark remains Pending and is excluded from the statistics and ranking.

Which AI platforms does the index cover?

This edition measures ChatGPT only. Results from other AI platforms are not included in the published score.

Does the index cover all licensed New York dispensaries?

No. The statistics describe the complete, published profiles in the measured sample shown on this page, not every licensed dispensary in New York State.

Can I request that a dispensary be added?

Yes. Contact Tarasovs Digital Agency to request inclusion in a future measurement batch.
