What we believe

Research before design.

We don’t open a design tool until we understand your users, your competitors, and your business goals. Every visual and structural decision we make is traceable to a reason. This makes our work more predictable and your investment more defensible.

Build for where search is going, not where it’s been.

The agencies still optimizing purely for Google 2020 are doing their clients a disservice. AI search is not a future trend — it’s a present reality. We take it seriously because our clients’ businesses depend on being discoverable wherever their customers are looking


Transparency over performance.

We report what actually happened — traffic, citations, conversions, what worked and what didn’t. If a strategy isn’t delivering, we say so and adjust. We’d rather have an honest conversation than a comfortable client relationship built on misleading reports


Character is non-negotiable.

Skill and experience are the minimum requirement. We hire people who take ownership, communicate proactively, and care about the outcome beyond their specific deliverable. A team of talented people with poor character produces poor results. We’ve built our team the other way around.


Quality over volume.

We take on fewer clients than we could, because every client gets senior attention. We’re not interested in scaling a model that waters down quality. We’re interested in building a reputation that compounds.

What we do
and why it's different

Most digital agencies offer web design, SEO, or both. We do the same — but with one additional layer that most agencies haven’t built yet: GEO, Generative Engine Optimization.

In 2026, a growing share of your potential clients don’t search Google. They ask ChatGPT. They use Perplexity. They get answers from Google AI Overviews without clicking a single link. If your business isn’t represented in those AI-generated responses, you’re invisible to a portion of your market that’s growing every month.

We build websites and digital strategies that work across all three discovery channels simultaneously:

Web Design & Development

Fast, conversion-focused websites built on clean semantic code, optimized for Core Web Vitals, and structured for both human readers and AI retrieval systems.

SEO

Technical SEO, content strategy, and local SEO for New York and target European markets. Rankings that compound over time, not paid placements that disappear when the budget stops.

GEO — AI Search Optimization

Structured content, FAQPage schema, E-E-A-T authority building, and Share of Model tracking. We help your brand appear in ChatGPT answers, Perplexity citations, and Google AI Overviews — where the next generation of clients is looking.
